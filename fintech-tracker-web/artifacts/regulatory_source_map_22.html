◆Change Classification Taxonomy

The Regulatory Change Impact Radar classifies every detected regulatory change across four severity levels. Classification is performed by the LangChain / Claude pipeline using structured prompt engineering with the criteria below. Human review is required for all Critical-classified changes before report distribution.

CRITICAL
  • Mandatory compliance by a defined date with penalty exposure > R5M or licence revocation risk
  • Requires fundamental system re-architecture or new capability build
  • Affects all customer accounts or all payment flows
  • SARB Prudential directive or FSCA enforcement notice
  • New Act or amendment with < 6-month implementation window
  • Response SLA: Impact assessment within 24 hours
HIGH
  • Mandatory compliance with 6–18 month window
  • Significant process or system changes required across 3+ teams
  • Penalty exposure R500K–R5M for non-compliance
  • Affects a major product line or customer segment
  • Requires board awareness and budget approval
  • Response SLA: Impact assessment within 72 hours
MEDIUM
  • Compliance required within 18 months or more
  • Process or policy updates required in 1–2 teams
  • Penalty exposure < R500K or reputational risk
  • Guidance note or interpretive communication
  • Updates to existing controls, not net-new capability
  • Response SLA: Impact assessment within 5 business days
LOW
  • Informational, consultation papers, or industry guidance
  • No mandatory compliance deadline
  • Future-watch items for strategic awareness
  • Minor policy clarification with no operational impact
  • International standards with voluntary adoption only
  • Response SLA: Monthly digest inclusion
