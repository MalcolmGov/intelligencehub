Section 01
Regulatory Context

Applicable regulatory frameworks and their relationship to the UC20 scanner.

Note: This document maps POPIA sections 8–25 (Processing conditions) to implementation approaches within the AI POPIA Compliance Scanner. The scanner both enforces these provisions in scanned codebases AND must itself comply with POPIA in its own operation. Both dimensions are addressed below.
🇿🇦
POPIA

Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013. Commenced 1 July 2021. Enforced by the Information Regulator (South Africa). Primary framework for the scanner's detection rules and output reports.

🏦
FSCA

Financial Sector Conduct Authority. Conduct Standard obligations for financial institutions. Scanner maps FSCA requirements for PII handling, consent in financial products, and customer data retention rules.

🏛
SARB

South African Reserve Bank. Guidance Note 5 on operational risk and IT risk. Prudential Standard PA 17. Scanner outputs feed SARB technology risk register and supervisory reporting requirements.

Section 03
Gap Analysis — Current State vs Target State

Identified gaps between current detection capabilities and full compliance coverage.

Gap Analysis Basis: Current state represents the scanner capabilities as designed at planning phase (UC20 v1.0). Target state represents full compliance with POPIA processing conditions and Information Regulator expectations. Gaps will be resolved during the 8h AI-assisted development sprint.
Gap ID POPIA Section Current State Target State Gap Description Priority Remediation Approach
GAP-001 § 10 Purpose Specification Scanner checks for @DataPurpose annotations only Detect all purpose specification patterns across Python/TS codebases No support for JSDoc @purpose tags, OpenAPI extensions, or inline comment-based purpose declarations. Only custom annotation supported. High Extend pattern library to cover JSDoc, OpenAPI x-data-purpose extension, and inline comment regex. Add TS AST parser for decorator patterns.
GAP-002 § 12 Indirect Collection Detects known data broker SDK domains Full third-party data flow tracing with provenance Domain allow/block list must be manually maintained. No automatic detection of novel or unlisted data brokers. No cross-service data lineage tracing. High Integrate with network policy analysis. Add ML classifier for novel data broker API patterns. Implement cross-service data lineage via OpenTelemetry trace analysis.
GAP-003 § 14 Information Quality Detects presence/absence of validation decorators Validate correctness of validation logic (e.g., SA ID checksum algorithm) Scanner flags missing validation but cannot verify that the validation logic is correct (e.g., a field has @Validates but the validator is a no-op or incorrect implementation). Medium Add symbolic execution for common validation functions. Unit test validator correctness with SA-specific test vectors. Flag validators with 0% code coverage in test reports.
GAP-004 § 19 Operator / DPA Flags integrations without DPA config reference Verify DPA is current, signed and covers actual processing activities Scanner cannot verify that a referenced DPA document actually exists, is signed, covers the relevant processing activities or has not expired. Reference only — no document validation. Medium Integrate with document management system (SharePoint/Confluence) to verify DPA document existence and expiry date. Add DPA validity check to report.
GAP-005 § 23 Data Subject Access Detects SAR endpoint presence Verify SAR response completeness and identity verification strength Scanner confirms a /sar or /data-access endpoint exists but cannot verify that the response is complete (covers all PII held), delivered within the 30-day period, or that identity verification is sufficiently robust. High Add SAR response schema validation. Check for response deadline tracking mechanism. Verify identity verification strength (MFA required for SAR fulfillment).
GAP-006 § 24 Correction Right Detects PATCH/PUT on PII models Verify correction propagation to downstream systems and third parties Scanner detects correction endpoints but cannot verify that corrections are propagated to all downstream systems, third-party processors and partner integrations that hold copies of the personal information. Medium Trace data flow from correction endpoint through all downstream event publishers. Check for correction propagation events in message queues. Flag orphaned data copies.
GAP-007 § 25 Erasure / Right to Object Detects DELETE endpoints and anonymisation jobs Verify cascading erasure across all data stores including backups Scanner confirms erasure endpoint exists but cannot verify that the erasure cascades to: read replicas, data warehouses, backups, analytics stores, and third-party copies held by operators/partners. High Implement data store inventory from infrastructure-as-code (Terraform/Bicep). Cross-check erasure job against all known data stores. Flag data stores not covered by erasure procedure.

Section 06
Compliance Evidence Artifacts

Artifacts produced by the scanner to constitute the compliance evidence pack.

📦
Auto-generated Evidence Artifacts
  • 📊
    POPIA Gap Report (HTML/PDF)
    Per-scan findings report with POPIA section references, severity, responsible team and remediation guidance. Signed and archived to evidence store on each scan.
  • 🗺
    Living Data Flow Diagram (SVG/JSON)
    Auto-updated DFD showing PII flows through all microservices. Versioned per scan. Aligned to IR DFD format requirements. Exportable as PDF for regulatory submission.
  • 📋
    Record of Processing Activities (ROPA)
    Auto-updated ROPA derived from code-level data annotations. Includes: purpose, lawful basis, categories of data subjects, recipients, retention periods. GDPR Art. 30 aligned.
  • 📝
    Scan Audit Log
    Immutable log of every scan invocation: who triggered, repo, branch, timestamp, findings count by severity, report download events. Retained 3 years per § 55.
  • 🎫
    Jira Remediation Ticket Export
    Snapshot of all auto-created remediation tickets with status, assignee, due date and resolution notes. Demonstrates corrective action management to IR.
  • 🏛
    Section 22 Notification Record
    Complete record of all breach notifications: draft, sent copy, IR delivery receipt, 72h timer, DPO sign-off. Retained 5 years for regulatory purposes.
📁
Manual Evidence Artifacts (DPO Maintained)
  • 📜
    Information Regulator Registration
    IR registration certificate as responsible party. Reference number referenced in scanner config. Annual renewal tracked.
  • 🤝
    Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
    Signed DPAs with all third-party processors identified by scanner. DPA reference linked in scanner config per integration. Expiry date monitored.
  • 👔
    DPO Appointment Record
    Formal DPO appointment documentation. DPO contact details referenced in all scanner-generated notifications. IR notification of appointment.
  • 📚
    POPIA Training Records
    Developer POPIA awareness training completion records. Required before scan result access is granted. Completion verified by IdP role assignment.
  • 🔍
    Annual Compliance Self-Assessment
    Annual review of scanner effectiveness against POPIA processing conditions. Produced by DPO using scanner trend data. Submitted to Board Risk Committee.
  • 📊
    FSCA / SARB Regulatory Submissions
    Quarterly compliance posture exports in FSCA and SARB prescribed formats. Derived from scanner aggregate data. Reviewed and signed by Malcolm Govender before submission.
