Executive Summary

South African financial institutions operate in one of the world's most demanding regulatory environments — POPIA, FSCA, SARB and FICA create overlapping obligations around data minimisation, consent, customer due diligence and fraud prevention. Yet the systems that hold customer data remain deeply fragmented: a customer's banking profile, call-centre history, transactional behaviour, web interactions and KYC records live in six or more isolated platforms with no unified identity layer connecting them.

This fragmentation is not merely an operational inconvenience — it is a strategic liability. Relationship managers lack context. Fraud models operate on incomplete signals. Cross-sell and next-best-action engines fire against stale, partial data. POPIA consent obligations cannot be reliably tracked or enforced across systems. The result: missed revenue, elevated regulatory exposure and a customer experience that feels disjointed despite significant technology investment.

UC-24 establishes the AI Customer Intelligence Layer — a unified platform that resolves fragmented identities across all source systems, assembles a continuously-updated Unified Customer Profile, enriches it with real-time behavioural signals, and powers AI-driven next-best-action recommendations accessible across every customer-facing touchpoint. It integrates bi-directionally with ARIA (UC18, voice interaction) and PayGuard (UC16, fraud risk) to share and consume signals in real time. POPIA consent is tracked natively, with right-to-erasure propagation engineered into the core data model from day one.

Malcolm Govender identified this gap following a cross-system data audit that revealed customers appearing under different identities in six source systems with no shared key, consent records scattered across three platforms, and the fraud model operating without 40% of available behavioural signals. The business case for a unified intelligence layer is clear and urgent.

Problem Statement

The core problem is that no single source of truth for customer identity or behaviour exists across the organisation. The following six siloed systems each hold an incomplete, often inconsistent picture of the same customer:

🏦
Core Banking
Account data, balances, KYC — no behavioural signals
📞
Telephony / IVR
Call records, voice interactions — no profile linkage
🎫
Support CRM
Tickets, complaints — siloed from transactions
🌐
Web / App
Digital behaviour — anonymous, not linked to customer ID
💳
Transactions
Payment history — no consent or preference context
📋
KYC / FICA
Identity documents — no linkage to behaviour or consent
No Unified Customer View — Relationship Managers Operate Blind
Relationship managers must manually cross-reference three to five systems to assemble a customer picture before any meaningful interaction. This adds 8–12 minutes of preparation per customer engagement, degrades conversation quality and creates errors when different systems show conflicting information. Next-best-action recommendations are impossible without a unified profile to reason over.
POPIA Consent Gaps — Material Regulatory Exposure
Consent records are maintained independently in three separate systems with no synchronisation mechanism. When a customer withdraws consent in one system, this change does not propagate to others. POPIA Section 11 (lawful basis) and Section 24 (right to object/withdraw) obligations cannot be reliably met. The estimated POPIA exposure from known consent gaps exceeds R10 million in potential administrative penalties under Section 107.
Fraud Models Operating on Incomplete Signals
PayGuard (UC16) fraud risk scoring operates primarily on transaction data, without access to behavioural signals from web and app interactions, call-centre patterns or cross-channel velocity. Industry benchmarks show that multi-channel behavioural signals improve fraud detection precision by 25–40%. This integration gap represents both a financial loss and a false-positive cost to legitimate customers.
Identity Resolution — Six Systems, No Shared Key
A single customer may appear as up to six distinct records across source systems — different name variants, multiple phone numbers, historical ID numbers and various email addresses. Without a probabilistic entity resolution engine, these records cannot be confidently linked, making unified profiling impossible and duplicate-contact compliance reporting unreliable.
Cross-Sell Conversion Severely Constrained by Data Fragmentation
Product recommendation engines fire against stale, single-system data. Customers receive irrelevant offers because the recommendation model lacks visibility into recent complaints, product holdings in other divisions, or shifts in transaction behaviour indicating new financial needs. Cross-sell conversion rates remain significantly below achievable benchmarks.
Manual Compliance Effort — Data Subject Requests Cannot Be Fulfilled Efficiently
POPIA Section 23 (right of access) and Section 24 (right to erasure/correction) requests currently require manual investigation across all six source systems. A single data subject access request takes 3–5 business days and involves four teams. POPIA mandates 30-day fulfilment. Without a unified identity layer and automated propagation, this obligation remains operationally fragile.

Solution Overview

The AI Customer Intelligence Layer is delivered in three structured phases, each building on the last. Phase 1 establishes the identity and data foundation. Phase 2 adds the AI intelligence layer. Phase 3 integrates with the broader ARIA and PayGuard ecosystem and enables the customer-facing 360 view.

Phase 1
Data Stitching & Unified Profile
Entity resolution engine. Unified Customer Profile store. POPIA consent model. Multi-source ingestion pipeline. Right-to-erasure propagation.
Phase 2
AI Intelligence Layer
Real-time behavioural signal ingestion. Next-best-action recommendation engine. Propensity models. Churn prediction. Sentiment enrichment.
Phase 3
System Integrations
ARIA (UC18) voice interaction integration. PayGuard (UC16) fraud signal sharing. Admin 360 dashboard. Audit trail. API surface for all consumers.
Target State
Single Unified Customer Profile — <200ms API Response
One API call returns a complete, consent-verified, AI-enriched customer profile stitched from all source systems — accessible by any authorised internal consumer in real time

Core Capabilities

Probabilistic Entity Resolution Engine
Multi-attribute matching across name variants (Soundex / phonetic normalisation), RSA ID number, passport number, all known phone numbers (mobile/landline), email addresses and account numbers. Confidence scoring with human-review queue for ambiguous matches. Golden record assembly from highest-confidence attributes across sources.
Unified Customer Profile Store
Event-sourced profile store maintaining full history of all attribute changes with source attribution and timestamp. Profile schema includes: identity attributes, product holdings, financial behaviour summary, communication preferences, consent records, risk profile, AI-generated propensity scores and next-best-action signals. POPIA-compliant data minimisation enforced at schema level.
POPIA-Native Consent Management
Purpose-specific consent records stored with lawful basis, collection date, channel, expiry and withdrawal state. Consent changes propagate automatically to all source systems via integration bus. Right-to-erasure triggers coordinated deletion across all linked records with audit confirmation. Data subject request workflow automated end-to-end.
Real-Time Behavioural Signal Ingestion
Event streaming pipeline consuming signals from web/app clickstream, IVR interaction events, transaction events and support ticket state changes. Sub-second signal ingestion latency. Behavioural features computed incrementally and merged into the live profile. Signals used to update propensity scores and next-best-action recommendations in near real time.
AI Next-Best-Action Recommendation Engine
Ensemble model combining collaborative filtering, propensity scoring and contextual bandits to generate ranked product and service recommendations. Recommendations contextualised by channel (voice, digital, branch), recency of behavioural signals and customer lifecycle stage. POPIA consent verified before any personalised recommendation is surfaced.
Bi-Directional ARIA (UC18) Integration
ARIA voice assistant queries the Unified Customer Profile API to contextualise voice interactions with full customer history, product holdings and sentiment profile. AI layer returns next-best-action recommendations to ARIA at call time. Post-call sentiment and outcome signals fed back into the profile for continuous enrichment.
Bi-Directional PayGuard (UC16) Integration
PayGuard fraud risk engine subscribes to real-time behavioural signals from the intelligence layer — web velocity, IVR anomalies and cross-channel authentication patterns. In return, PayGuard fraud risk scores are written back into the Unified Customer Profile for use by relationship managers and recommendation engine context filtering.

Technical Architecture

The AI Customer Intelligence Layer is composed of six primary components connected via an asynchronous event integration bus. The architecture is designed for horizontal scalability, event-driven enrichment and strict data access control.

Entity Resolution Engine (Python)
Probabilistic record linkage using blocking strategies (Soundex, phonetic normalisation, RSA ID normalisation) followed by pairwise comparison with weighted attribute scoring. Splink or custom scikit-learn model for match probability. Confidence bands: >85% auto-merge, 60–85% human review queue, <60% no match. Golden Record assembly merges highest-confidence attributes with source attribution retained.
Unified Profile Store (Python + TypeScript)
Event-sourced profile store built on PostgreSQL with Redis caching layer for hot profile reads. Profile schema versioned with forward/backward compatibility. POPIA data minimisation enforced at write time — only permitted fields stored per purpose. Full event history retained for audit reconstruction. Consent state stored as first-class profile attribute, not a sidecar.
Behavioural Signal Ingestion Pipeline (Python)
Apache Kafka or AWS Kinesis event streaming backbone consuming events from six source systems. Schema Registry enforces event contract versioning. Stream processors (Faust / AWS Lambda) compute incremental behavioural features and write enriched signals to the profile store. Dead-letter queue with alerting for failed event processing. Sub-5-second end-to-end latency target.
AI Recommendation & Propensity Engine (Python)
Ensemble model: collaborative filtering (matrix factorisation) combined with XGBoost propensity scoring and a contextual bandit layer for channel-specific recommendation ranking. Models retrained weekly on updated profile and behavioural data. POPIA consent gate enforced pre-inference — no personalised output generated without verified marketing consent. Model outputs stored back in profile for API access.
Integration Bus & Consent Propagation (Python)
Event-driven integration bus managing bi-directional message flow between the intelligence layer, six source systems, ARIA (UC18) and PayGuard (UC16). Consent withdrawal events published as priority messages with guaranteed delivery and receipt acknowledgement from each target system. Integration health dashboard shows per-system delivery latency and error rates. Circuit breaker pattern applied per integration endpoint.
API Layer & Access Control (TypeScript / FastAPI)
RESTful Profile API with OpenAPI 3.0 specification. OAuth 2.0 / JWT authentication. Caller identity bound to purpose claim at token issuance time — field-level filtering applied per purpose at query time. Rate limiting per caller. Distributed caching (Redis) for hot profiles. Audit log write on every API call (async, does not add to response latency). API gateway with mTLS for internal service-to-service calls.
Admin Dashboard & Customer 360 View (TypeScript / React)
Single-page application providing Customer 360 view, POPIA compliance monitoring, entity resolution management interface and audit log search. Role-based access control with four roles: RM (read-only profile view), Data Steward (entity resolution, consent management), Compliance Officer (POPIA reporting, erasure workflows) and System Admin (full access). All dashboard actions logged in the audit trail. Built as an internal web application, not exposed externally.

Next Steps

Phase 1 — Foundation Audit & Design (Weeks 1–4)

  1. Conduct cross-system data audit — inventory all customer-related data fields in each of the six source systems; document current identity keys, consent record locations and data quality issues
  2. Design entity resolution matching strategy — define attribute weighting model, blocking strategy and confidence threshold bands; establish human review queue workflow and tooling
  3. Define Unified Customer Profile schema — agree field set, consent model structure, data minimisation rules and purpose taxonomy with compliance, legal and business stakeholders
  4. Establish POPIA consent baseline — identify all current consent records across three systems; assess gaps, conflicts and expired consents; define migration strategy for Day 1 consent state

Phase 2 — Core Build (Weeks 5–12)

  1. Build and validate entity resolution engine — train on historical customer records with known ground truth; achieve >90% precision on test set before production ingestion begins
  2. Deploy event-sourced Unified Customer Profile store — initial population from core banking system as the highest-quality source; progressively merge additional source systems
  3. Implement POPIA consent management module — consent record migration, withdrawal propagation pipeline, erasure workflow and data subject access request automation
  4. Build behavioural signal ingestion pipeline — connect to web/app, IVR and transaction event streams; validate sub-5-second end-to-end latency under load
  5. Develop and validate Unified Customer Profile API — performance testing to confirm P95 <200ms target; field-level access control integration testing with purpose-claim enforcement

Phase 3 — AI Layer & Integrations (Weeks 13–20)

  1. Train and deploy next-best-action recommendation engine — A/B test against existing recommendation approach; measure cross-sell conversion uplift in controlled cohort before full rollout
  2. Deliver ARIA (UC18) bi-directional integration — coordinate with UC18 team on API contract; validate <200ms profile context response at call initiation; test post-call writeback
  3. Deliver PayGuard (UC16) bi-directional integration — coordinate on behavioural signal schema; validate fraud risk score writeback to profile; measure improvement in fraud signal coverage
  4. Launch Customer 360 admin dashboard — pilot with a cohort of relationship managers; gather structured feedback on profile completeness, recommendation relevance and POPIA workflow usability
  5. Complete compliance validation — conduct POPIA readiness assessment with legal; verify all Sections 8–25 obligations met; produce audit evidence pack for FSCA and SARB review readiness
