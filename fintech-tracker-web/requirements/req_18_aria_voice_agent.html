Executive Summary & Business Case

MTN Group Fintech and its banking partners field tens of thousands of inbound support calls per month across South Africa alone. The L1 support function — handling password resets, account status queries, basic product information, and P3/P4 incident triage — accounts for an estimated 65% of total contact-centre volume. At an average handling time of 7.2 minutes and a fully-loaded agent cost of R420/hour, the annual L1 support cost across fintech and banking products exceeds R18 million in South Africa before considering attrition, training and quality assurance overhead.

ARIA (AI Response & Intelligence Agent) is a voice-first AI L1 support agent designed to resolve the majority of these interactions autonomously, with natural, human-quality voice output synthesised by ElevenLabs voice cloning technology. The system answers inbound calls via a Twilio integration, identifies the caller, determines their intent, retrieves answers from a RAG-powered banking and IT knowledge base, resolves L1 queries end-to-end, and escalates only genuine L2/L3 issues to human agents — with full context passed in the handoff.

At 94% completion, ARIA is in the final stages of production deployment. The remaining work centres on sourcing African English and French voice recordings for voice cloning, and executing the ElevenLabs production deployment pipeline. The projected saving of $400k–$800k per year is derived from a 60% L1 deflection rate applied to current contact-centre volumes, with a conservative 40% cost-per-call reduction accounting for human-agent supervision of edge cases.

Problem Statement

Three compounding problems drive the business case for ARIA:

Problem 1 — L1 Support Costs Are Unsustainable at Scale
As MTN Fintech expands its banking product suite and customer base, L1 support volumes grow linearly with customer acquisition. Human agent headcount cannot scale economically. Call centre attrition in South Africa averages 35% annually, creating perpetual training cycles. The unit economics of human L1 support break the business case for financial inclusion products targeting lower-income segments where ARPU is low.
Problem 2 — Existing IVR Systems Are Rigid and Frustrating
Current IVR systems present pre-recorded menu trees that cannot understand natural language, cannot look up customer-specific information in real time, and cannot resolve queries end-to-end. Customers are routed to human agents for queries that are entirely resolvable from structured knowledge bases. The IVR-to-agent transfer rate is 78%, meaning the IVR is functioning as a delay mechanism rather than a resolution channel.
Problem 3 — P1/P2 Incident Triage Is Slow Without Intelligent Routing
When critical incidents occur (P1/P2), the absence of intelligent triage means high-severity reports are mixed with low-priority queries in the same queue. Mean time to acknowledge (MTTA) for P1 incidents averages 18 minutes due to queue saturation. ARIA classifies incident severity in real time during the call, immediately escalating P1/P2 calls to the on-call engineer with structured context, targeting MTTA under 3 minutes.

User Stories

US-01 · Password Reset
Self-Service Password / PIN Reset via Voice
"As a MTN banking customer, I want to reset my internet banking password over the phone without waiting for a human agent."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ARIA identifies caller by MSISDN and account verification
  • Password reset OTP flow completed end-to-end without agent
  • Confirmation SMS sent to registered number after reset
  • Full call transcript logged with PII masked for compliance
US-02 · P1 Incident Triage
Critical Incident Escalated Immediately
"As an IT engineer, I want ARIA to classify my incident report as P1 and immediately connect me to the on-call team with my context pre-loaded."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ARIA classifies incident as P1 within first 60 seconds of call
  • On-call engineer notified via PagerDuty within 90 seconds
  • Structured incident summary passed in escalation payload
  • Caller connected to on-call engineer or bridge within 3 minutes total
US-03 · Zulu Language Support
Native Zulu Voice Interaction
"As a Zulu-speaking MTN customer, I want to speak to ARIA in Zulu and receive natural voice responses in Zulu."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Language detected as Zulu with ≥97% accuracy within 5 seconds
  • Voice response in cloned Zulu-accented voice
  • Banking knowledge base queried in Zulu context
  • Escalation handoff to Zulu-speaking agent if needed
US-04 · Product Query Resolution
Banking Product Information via RAG
"As a customer enquiring about home loan rates, I want ARIA to retrieve current rate information accurately without hallucinating."
Acceptance Criteria
  • RAG retrieves relevant document chunk with source citation
  • ARIA quotes current rate accurately from knowledge base
  • Response generated in under 4 seconds end-to-end
  • Offer to send information via SMS at end of interaction
US-05 · Compliance Query
FICA / Regulatory Document Query
"As a compliance officer, I want to ask ARIA about FICA requirements for a specific product and get a sourced answer."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Compliance KB includes FICA, POPIA, and FSCA documents
  • RAG retrieves correct regulatory passage with section reference
  • ARIA states the source document and section clearly
  • Option to escalate to human compliance officer if uncertain
US-06 · Graceful Escalation
L2 Escalation with Full Context Transfer
"As a human agent receiving an escalated call, I want to see a structured summary of what ARIA discussed so I don't repeat the customer's journey."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Escalation payload includes: caller ID, intent, conversation summary, KB articles referenced, sentiment score
  • Agent screen-pop populated before call connects
  • Call recording and transcript available in CRM immediately
  • Customer not asked to repeat information already given to ARIA
US-07 · POPIA Consent
Voice Recording Consent at Call Start
"As a customer, I want to be told that my call may be recorded and processed by AI before any personal data is collected."
Acceptance Criteria
  • POPIA consent notice delivered within first 10 seconds of call
  • Customer can opt out of AI processing and route to human agent
  • Consent response (yes/no) recorded with timestamp in audit log
  • No personal data processed before consent obtained
US-08 · After-Hours Support
24/7 L1 Resolution Outside Business Hours
"As a customer with a banking issue at 2am, I want to resolve my query without waiting until 8am when human agents are available."
Acceptance Criteria
  • ARIA operates 24/7/365 without scheduled downtime
  • L1 resolvable queries (balance, statement, PIN reset) available at any hour
  • P1/P2 escalations routed to on-call engineer regardless of hour
  • L2/L3 non-urgent queries create ticket for next business day

Technical Architecture

Voice Channel
Caller Phone → PSTN → Twilio Programmable Voice → Twilio Flex (for escalation) → ARIA Webhook Endpoint (FastAPI, Python)
STT / NLU Layer
Twilio <Gather> with Speech Recognition → Deepgram ASR (Nova-2, multilingual) → Language Detector → Intent Classifier (Claude claude-sonnet-4-6 via Anthropic API)
RAG & Knowledge Layer
Pinecone Vector DB (SA-region) → Embedding: text-embedding-3-large → Retrieval (top-3, similarity threshold 0.72) → LLM Response Generation (Claude claude-sonnet-4-6)
TTS & Voice Synthesis
ElevenLabs API (streaming TTS, cloned SA voices per language) → Audio stream → Twilio <Play> or <Stream> → Caller
Integrations & Ops
PagerDuty (P1/P2 alerts) · Jira/ServiceNow (ticket creation) · Slack (team notifications) · Kafka (audit log) · Prometheus + Grafana (monitoring) · SA-resident S3 (call recordings)

Deployment Pipeline

Production Deployment Command
The final production deployment is executed via: python elevenlabs_deploy.py --env prod. This script provisions the cloned voice profiles to the ElevenLabs production workspace, updates the Twilio webhook URLs, runs smoke tests across all 5 language packs, and verifies end-to-end call flow with synthetic test callers before enabling live traffic routing.

Data Flow & Security

Inbound Call Processing Pipeline
1
Caller dials MTN Fintech support number. Twilio receives call, triggers ARIA webhook. POPIA consent notice delivered within 10 seconds.
2
Caller speech captured via Twilio <Gather>. Deepgram ASR transcribes audio. Language detection runs on transcript.
3
Claude intent classifier categorises the turn. RAG retrieval queries Pinecone with the caller's intent as a search query.
4
Top-3 document chunks retrieved. Claude generates a grounded response using retrieved context. Response capped at 2–3 sentences for voice.
5
ElevenLabs synthesises response in caller's detected language with cloned SA voice. Audio streamed back via Twilio.
6
Conversation continues until: resolution confirmed, escalation triggered (P1/P2), or max turns reached (8 turns before escalation offer).
7
Call recording and full transcript logged to SA-resident S3. PII masked in transcript. Audit record created in Kafka topic.

Data & AI Model Requirements

Knowledge Base Content

Document Categories
  • Banking product FAQs (loans, accounts, cards, forex)
  • IT support runbooks (password reset, access issues, system errors)
  • FICA, POPIA, FSCA regulatory documents (full text)
  • Incident response procedures (P1–P4 playbooks)
  • Fee and tariff schedules per product
  • Contact escalation directories
  • MTN MoMo integration guides for banking staff
Embedding & Retrieval Configuration
Embedding model: OpenAI text-embedding-3-large (3072 dimensions). Chunk size: 512 tokens with 64-token overlap. Vector store: Pinecone (SA-region index). Retrieval: top-3 chunks, MMR reranking, similarity threshold 0.72. Metadata filters: document type, effective date, product category.

Voice Cloning Requirements

ElevenLabs Voice Clone Specifications
  • SA English: 2 native SA English speakers, 30+ min audio each
  • Zulu: 2 native Zulu speakers (female + male), 25+ min each
  • Xhosa: 2 native Xhosa speakers, 25+ min each
  • Afrikaans: 2 native Afrikaans speakers, 25+ min each
  • French: 2 West African French speakers, 25+ min each
Next Step: Voice Recording Sourcing
Voice recordings for Zulu, Xhosa, Afrikaans, and French are the primary blocker for production deployment. Recordings must be sourced from native speakers with signed recording consent forms. Professional studio quality (16kHz+, no background noise) required for ElevenLabs cloning quality threshold.

POPIA Audit Trail Requirements
Every ARIA interaction SHALL produce an immutable audit record containing: caller MSISDN (hashed), call start/end timestamps, consent decision (accept/decline), language detected, intent classifications per turn, KB articles retrieved (document IDs), escalation trigger (if any), and call recording reference.
