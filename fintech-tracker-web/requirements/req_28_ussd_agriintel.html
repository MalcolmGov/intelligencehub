1. Executive Summary & Business Case

Smallholder farmers constitute the backbone of food security across sub-Saharan Africa, cultivating an estimated 80% of the continent's farmland on plots smaller than 2 hectares. Yet these farmers operate under a profound information asymmetry: commodity prices in their nearest market town may differ by 40–80% from prices 50 kilometres away, weather forecasts are inaccessible without a smartphone or radio, and pest or disease outbreaks spread unchecked because farmers have no early-warning advisory system. The result is chronic income underperformance — not because these farmers lack skill or land, but because they lack market intelligence.

MTN Group serves farmers in all 13 of its African markets, predominantly via 2G USSD on basic feature phones. UC-28 — USSD AgriIntel — transforms MTN's network into an agricultural intelligence platform: commodity prices for 50+ crops updated three times daily from market aggregators, hyperlocal 7-day weather forecasts from meteorological API partners, an AI crop advisor that delivers season-appropriate guidance, and a buyer-seller matching marketplace where farmers can list produce and connect directly with verified buyers via USSD and SMS.

The projected impact is a 20–30% increase in farmer income within the engaged user cohort — validated by comparable deployments: Esoko in Ghana delivered 25% income uplift, Reuters Market Light in India delivered 16–20%, and iCOW in Kenya improved cattle farmer income by 32%. For MTN, AgriIntel creates a high-engagement daily-use product that drives MoMo transaction volume (farmers transact proceeds through MoMo), airtime revenue, and positions MTN as a socially impactful financial inclusion leader across agriculture-dependent markets.

2. Problem Statement

Problem 1 — Price Information Asymmetry Costs Farmers 40–80% of Potential Income
A smallholder farmer in rural Ghana selling maize to the first trader who arrives at their farm gate has no way of knowing that the market price in Accra is 60% higher than what the trader is offering. This information asymmetry — which traders exploit systematically — is the single largest cause of preventable income loss for African smallholders. Access to daily commodity prices in multiple markets would fundamentally shift negotiating power from intermediaries to farmers.
Problem 2 — Weather Forecast Inaccessibility Causes Avoidable Crop Loss
Smallholder farmers in sub-Saharan Africa have no reliable access to hyperlocal weather forecasts. National meteorological services publish forecasts at district level with 24–48 hour lag. Farmers plant, irrigate, spray pesticides, and harvest based on historical patterns and intuition — not forecast data. A 7-day hyperlocal forecast would enable smarter irrigation scheduling, early harvest before rain damage, and planting timing optimisation. In pilot studies, weather-informed farmers reduced weather-related crop loss by 15–25%.
Problem 3 — Fragmented Supply Chains Lock Out Smallholders from Premium Markets
Large buyers (supermarkets, food processors, exporters) cannot efficiently source from thousands of small individual farmers. Smallholders cannot access these premium markets. The gap is informational, not logistical: buyers do not know who has produce available, in what quantity, and at what location. A USSD-based produce listing and buyer-matching system connects buyers with aggregated smallholder supply, enabling premium market access without requiring a smartphone or internet connection.

4. User Stories

US-01 · Commodity Price Check
Farmer Checks Current Maize Price
"As a maize farmer in Ghana, I want to check the current price of maize in 3 nearby markets before I sell to a trader."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Customer selects crop (maize) and region via USSD menu
  • Prices shown for nearest 3 markets (name + price per kg/bag)
  • Price freshness shown ("Updated 4h ago")
  • All within 160-char USSD format with pagination
US-02 · Weather Forecast
Farmer Gets 7-Day Hyperlocal Forecast
"As a farmer preparing to plant, I want a 7-day weather forecast for my specific area to plan my planting date."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Farmer's district/location stored at registration
  • 7-day forecast shown: daily condition + rain probability
  • Paginated across 2–3 USSD screens
  • Forecast updated daily from meteorological API partner
US-03 · AI Crop Advisory
Crop-Specific Growing Advice
"As a tomato farmer, I want advice on what to do with my crop this week given current weather conditions."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Farmer selects crop and growth stage via USSD
  • Advisory generated combining: crop stage, current weather forecast, seasonal calendar
  • Advisory le 3 paginated USSD screens
  • Advisory in farmer's registered language
US-04 · Produce Listing
Farmer Lists Produce for Sale
"As a farmer with 10 bags of cassava ready, I want to list it for sale on the USSD marketplace so buyers can contact me."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Farmer enters: crop, quantity, asking price, location, available from date
  • Listing posted to AgriIntel marketplace within 60 seconds
  • SMS sent to farmer confirming listing reference number
  • Listing visible to registered buyers searching that crop and region
US-05 · Buyer Matching
Buyer Searches for Available Produce
"As a food trader, I want to search for available cassava in Brong-Ahafo region via USSD and contact matching farmers."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Buyer selects crop and region; sees top 3 matching listings
  • Each listing shows: quantity, price, distance, listing date
  • Buyer can initiate contact via USSD-triggered SMS to farmer
  • Farmer receives SMS with buyer contact details within 2 minutes
US-06 · Pest Alert Subscription
Farmer Receives Crop Disease Alert
"As a maize farmer, I want to receive an SMS alert if Fall Armyworm is reported in my district so I can act early."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Farmer subscribes to pest alerts for registered crop and region
  • Pest reports from agricultural extension services trigger SMS within 24h
  • Alert includes: pest name, severity, recommended action
  • Advisory on treatment available via USSD follow-up
US-07 · Price Alert
Automated Price Alert When Target Price Reached
"As a farmer holding cassava in storage, I want an SMS alert when the cassava price in Accra reaches R2.50/kg."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Farmer sets price alert via USSD: crop, market, target price
  • SMS sent within 4 hours of price reaching target
  • Alert includes current price and 3-day trend
  • Maximum 3 active price alerts per farmer
US-08 · Hausa Language Support (Nigeria)
Full AgriIntel Experience in Hausa
"As a Hausa-speaking farmer in Kano State, I want to access commodity prices and crop advice entirely in Hausa."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Hausa menu strings, crop names, and advisory text localised
  • Nigerian commodity prices (sorghum, millet, groundnut, cowpea)
  • Weather forecast for Northern Nigeria regions
  • All within 160-char Hausa text constraints

6. Technical Architecture

USSD Channel
Farmer Feature Phone (2G) → USSD Gateway (per OpCo) → AgriIntel USSD App Server (FastAPI)  |  SMS Gateway (alerts + marketplace notifications)
Data Ingestion
Commodity Price APIs (ESOKO, national market data systems) → Weather APIs (AccuWeather / OpenWeatherMap per market) → Pest Alert Feeds (CABI, FAO EMPRES) → Kafka ingestion → Price and Weather Store (PostgreSQL + Redis cache)
AI Advisory Engine
Crop Advisory LLM (Claude claude-sonnet-4-6) + Agricultural Knowledge Base (RAG, Pinecone) → Context Builder (crop + weather + season + pest) → 160-char Response Formatter → Multilingual Output (8 languages)
Marketplace
Produce Listing Service (PostgreSQL) → Buyer Search Engine (location + crop matching) → Contact Bridge (SMS gateway, MoMo MSISDN) → Listing Expiry Manager (14-day TTL)
Ops & Compliance
Farmer Profile Store (per-market data residency) → Audit Log (Kafka) → Price Freshness Monitor → Grafana Dashboard → POPIA / NDPR Consent Store
