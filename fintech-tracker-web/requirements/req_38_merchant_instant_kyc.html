1. Executive Summary & Business Case

MTN Group Fintech's merchant acquisition engine is severely constrained by a KYC onboarding process that takes 3–7 business days to complete. A prospective merchant who walks up to a MoMo agent, expresses interest in accepting MoMo payments, and submits their documents, then waits almost a week before they can transact. In that window, the majority of leads go cold, switch to a competitor payment platform, or simply give up. Internal analysis indicates a current merchant onboarding conversion rate of 28–35% — meaning 65–72% of interested merchants never become active.

UC-38 eliminates this conversion barrier by reducing merchant KYC onboarding from days to under 3 minutes, entirely within the MoMo merchant app. The merchant points their smartphone camera at their South African ID book, Nigerian NIN card, Kenyan Huduma number card, or any of 30+ supported identity document types. The AI extracts all data fields using OCR and NLP, performs liveness detection to confirm the person holding the document is present and alive, cross-validates extracted data against national identity registries in real-time, validates business registration with CIPC (SA), CAC (Nigeria), or equivalent authorities, and calculates a risk score. The entire process takes under 3 minutes for compliant applicants.

The business case is compelling: a 3–5x improvement in merchant conversion rate directly translates to faster revenue growth, larger merchant network effects, and a stronger competitive position against super-apps. For every 1,000 merchant leads, the current process converts 300 merchants; UC-38 targets 900–1,500 merchants from the same lead volume. With average merchant annual GTV of R180,000, each incremental merchant represents approximately R5,400 in annual fee income at 3% merchant discount rate.

2. Problem Statement

Problem 1 — 3–7 Day KYC Latency Kills Merchant Conversion
The current merchant KYC process requires a merchant to physically present documents to a MoMo agent or field sales representative, wait 3–7 business days for back-office verification, and then receive a PIN via SMS before they can begin accepting payments. The majority of merchant leads lost between the expression of interest and activation are attributable to this latency, not to a lack of genuine intent. A street merchant cannot wait 7 days to start accepting digital payments — they need to accept a payment from their first customer today.
Problem 2 — Manual KYC Is Expensive and Does Not Scale
Each manually processed KYC application costs MTN approximately R85–R120 in agent time, back-office review, and follow-up. Across 13 markets with hundreds of thousands of merchant applications annually, manual KYC represents a significant operational cost that scales linearly with growth. AI-powered instant KYC converts this variable cost into a fixed infrastructure cost, enabling 10x merchant volume growth without proportional cost increase. The per-application cost drops to approximately R2–R8 for AI-automated processing.
Problem 3 — Manual KYC Has Inconsistent Quality Across 13 Markets
The quality of KYC verification varies significantly across MTN's 13 markets due to differences in agent training, documentation practices, and ID verification standards. Some markets have documented instances of agents accepting photocopied documents, failing to perform liveness checks, or incorrectly transcribing ID numbers. This creates both AML/CFT compliance risk and credit risk on the MoMo merchant book. AI-powered OCR and registry verification is both faster and more consistent than manual processing, providing an auditable, repeatable process that meets FATF standards across all markets.

4. AI Onboarding Flow — End-to-End

Merchant Instant KYC — Step-by-Step (Target: <3 Minutes)
1
Download & Business Information (30s): Merchant downloads MoMo merchant app. Enters: business name, business type, trading address, mobile number. System prefills MTN subscriber data if MSISDN already registered.
2
ID Document Capture (45s): Merchant is prompted to "point camera at your ID document." AI automatically detects document type (30+ types), frames capture, adjusts for lighting, and captures front and back in one guided flow. No scanning app needed — native camera API used.
3
OCR Extraction & Validation (15s): On-device pre-processing + cloud OCR extracts all identity fields (name, ID number, DOB, address, issue/expiry). AI validates field consistency (e.g., DOB matches age from photo). Merchant confirms extracted data on screen.
4
Liveness Detection (30s): Merchant is prompted to take a short selfie video (3–5 seconds). AI performs active liveness check: random head turn instructions to prevent photo/video spoofing. Face matched against ID document photo using biometric comparison.
5
Registry Validation (20s): Extracted ID number submitted to national identity registry API (Home Affairs SA, NIMC Nigeria, IPRS Kenya, or market equivalent). Business registration number validated against CIPC/CAC/equivalent. AML sanctions screening run in parallel.
6
Risk Scoring & Decision (10s): AI risk engine combines: registry validation result, liveness confidence score, document authenticity score, AML screening result, and geolocation data. Risk band assigned: Low / Medium / High. Low: instant approval. Medium: approve with enhanced monitoring. High: manual review queue.
7
Activation (30s): Low/Medium risk merchants: instant provisional activation. MoMo merchant account created. QR code and POS credentials issued immediately in-app. Merchant can accept first payment within 3 minutes of starting. High risk: "Application under review" with 24-hour SLA communicated.

5. User Stories

US-01 · Instant SA Merchant Onboarding
South African Merchant Onboards in Under 3 Minutes
"As a new merchant in Johannesburg, I want to start accepting MoMo payments today without waiting days for my application to be processed."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Smart ID card captured, OCR extracts all fields with >98% accuracy
  • Home Affairs registry validation returns result in <20 seconds
  • Liveness detection completes in <30 seconds
  • Full onboarding (steps 1–7) completes in <3 minutes for low-risk applicants
  • QR code and merchant credentials issued immediately upon approval
US-02 · Liveness Anti-Spoofing
AI Detects Photo/Video Spoofing Attacks
"As a compliance officer, I want the system to reliably reject fraudulent onboarding attempts using printed photos or pre-recorded videos."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Active liveness: random head turn instruction prevents static image attacks
  • False Accept Rate (FAR) <0.1% for photo/video spoof attacks
  • False Reject Rate (FRR) <5% for genuine applicants
  • Liveness failure triggers "application under manual review" not automatic rejection
  • Liveness check result logged for audit with confidence score
US-03 · Multi-Market Document Support
Nigerian Merchant Onboards with NIN
"As a Nigerian merchant, I want to use my NIN slip or NIN card to onboard as a MoMo merchant without needing a passport or driver's licence."
Acceptance Criteria
  • NIN slip and NIN card both supported as valid ID documents for Nigeria
  • NIMC API integration validates NIN number in real-time
  • CAC registration number validated for formal business registrations
  • OCR extracts NIN, name, DOB from NIN document with >96% accuracy
  • Full Nigeria onboarding flow completes in <3 minutes
US-04 · Business Registration Validation
AI Validates Formal Business Registration
"As a registered business owner, I want my CIPC registration to be verified automatically so I can get the verified business merchant tier with higher transaction limits."
Acceptance Criteria
  • CIPC API integration validates company registration number in real-time (SA)
  • Verified business status unlocks higher transaction limits and MoMo Business tier
  • Business name from CIPC pre-populated in merchant profile
  • Sole proprietors supported via ID-only KYC at lower transaction tier
  • CAC (Nigeria), BRELA (Tanzania) equivalents supported per market
US-05 · Low Lighting Document Capture
Reliable Capture in Real-World Conditions
"As a merchant onboarding in a busy market stall, I want the app to work even in bright outdoor sunlight or dim indoor lighting."
Acceptance Criteria
  • App provides real-time capture guidance: "Move to better light", "Hold steady", "Too close"
  • OCR accuracy >94% in challenging lighting conditions (high glare, low light)
  • Auto-flash option for dark environments
  • Multi-frame capture: best frame selected automatically from 5-frame burst
  • Manual upload fallback: merchant can upload photo from gallery if live capture fails 3x
US-06 · AML Sanctions Screening
Real-Time Sanctions and PEP Screening
"As a compliance officer, I want every merchant applicant screened against sanctions lists and PEP databases in real-time before account activation."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Extracted name and ID screened against UN, OFAC, EU, and local sanctions lists
  • PEP (Politically Exposed Person) screening against Dow Jones/Refinitiv database
  • Screening completes within 10 seconds as part of the registry validation step
  • Any sanctions hit: application blocked and escalated to FinCrime team immediately
  • All screening results logged with timestamp and list version for audit
US-07 · Manual Review Queue
High-Risk Applications Routed to Human Review
"As a compliance officer, I want high-risk applications to be queued for my review with all AI evidence surfaced so I can make a fast, informed decision."
Acceptance Criteria
  • High-risk band applications enter a priority review queue with 24-hour SLA
  • Review interface shows: captured document images, OCR output, liveness score, registry result, risk score breakdown (SHAP)
  • Reviewer can approve, reject, or request additional documents in-app
  • Merchant notified of review status with estimated wait time
  • Review decision and rationale logged for audit and model feedback
US-08 · Re-KYC for Existing Merchants
Periodic KYC Refresh for Existing Merchants
"As a compliance officer, I want existing merchants to be able to complete their periodic KYC refresh in-app without visiting an agent."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Re-KYC flow identical to initial onboarding; skips already-validated fields
  • In-app reminder sent 30 days before KYC expiry
  • Merchant account not suspended until 7 days after KYC expiry (grace period)
  • Re-KYC completion time target: <90 seconds (pre-filled data)
  • Re-KYC covers: ID document refresh, liveness re-check, registry re-validation

8. Data & AI Model Requirements

8.1 OCR Model Requirements

Document Classification Model
Input: Captured document image. Output: Document type (30+ classes) + confidence score. Accuracy target: >99% top-1 classification. Training data: 50,000+ labelled document images per document type. Inference time: <500ms on cloud.
Field Extraction OCR Model
Input: Classified document image + document template. Output: Structured field map (name, ID number, DOB, etc.) + confidence per field. Accuracy target: >96% field-level at controlled conditions. NLP post-processing for name standardisation across character sets (Arabic, Amharic, Latin).
Document Authenticity Model
Input: High-resolution document image. Output: Authenticity score (0–1) + tamper indicators. Target: >95% detection of colour-copier reproductions; >90% detection of digital manipulation. Trained on genuine vs tampered document pairs per document type.
Liveness Detection Model
ISO/IEC 30107-3 Level 2 compliant. Input: Selfie video (3–5s). Output: Liveness pass/fail + confidence score. FAR <0.1%, FRR <5%. Trained on diverse African demographic data to avoid demographic bias.
Face Matching Model
Input: Liveness-verified selfie + ID document photo. Output: Match score (0–1). TAR >95% at FMR <0.01%. Trained and validated on African demographic representation. Third-party model evaluation by accredited biometric testing lab required before deployment.
Risk Scoring Engine
Input: Scores from all pipeline steps + geolocation + sanctions result + registry result. Output: Composite risk score 0–1 + SHAP explainability + risk band. Gradient boosting model. Retrained quarterly on outcome data (merchant fraud rates). AUC >0.82 target.

8.2 Bias Monitoring & Demographic Fairness

AI models used in KYC processes that affect access to financial services must not exhibit demographic bias. MTN's Instant KYC models shall be evaluated for fairness across gender, age, and nationality sub-groups:

  • OCR accuracy shall not differ by more than 2pp across demographic sub-groups (gender, nationality)
  • Liveness detection FRR shall not differ by more than 3pp across skin tone categories (Fitzpatrick scale)
  • Face matching TAR shall be evaluated against all 13 market demographic profiles; model shall not be deployed if any sub-group performance falls >5pp below mean
  • Manual review referral rate shall be monitored per demographic sub-group; any group with referral rate >2x the mean triggers model review and potential recalibration
  • External bias audit by accredited third party required before deployment across all 13 markets

Biometric Data — Special Category Protection
Under POPIA and equivalent legislation in all 13 markets, biometric data (selfie images, facial templates) constitutes a special category of personal information requiring explicit consent, heightened protection, and strict purpose limitation. UC-38 implements: (1) explicit, granular consent for biometric processing before capture begins, (2) biometric data encrypted at rest and in transit, (3) facial templates purged within 30 days of successful KYC, (4) no secondary use of biometric data for any purpose other than identity verification, (5) annual biometric data audit by external auditor. This approach exceeds minimum legal requirements and is designed to build long-term merchant trust in MTN's data practices.
