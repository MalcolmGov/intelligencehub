1. Executive Summary & Business Case

Financial literacy and personalised financial guidance are luxury goods in Africa — available to the wealthy through private bankers and financial planners, but inaccessible to the 65 million MoMo customers who need guidance most. These customers make daily financial decisions — how much to save, when to recharge airtime, whether to send money or keep a buffer — without access to any data-driven insight about their own financial health.

UC-27 delivers Africa's first truly accessible AI financial advisor: a system that runs entirely on the USSD channel (any GSM handset, no internet, 160-character messages) and analyses each customer's MoMo transaction history to compute a personalised 0–100 Financial Health Score. Every week, the coach sends a personalised nudge via SMS reflecting what the data says about that specific customer's financial behaviour. Over USSD, customers can explore their scores, set savings goals, and get spending category breakdowns in their preferred language.

The business case operates on two levels. First, customers who engage with their financial health score are measurably more likely to increase MoMo activity: comparable programmes (M-Pesa's Savings Coach pilot, Orange Money's financial literacy SMS) show 25–35% uplift in monthly transaction volumes among engaged customers. Second, the coach creates a natural cross-sell surface for credit, insurance, and savings products — reducing customer acquisition cost for these high-margin products.

2. Problem Statement

Problem 1 — No Access to Personalised Financial Guidance
The 65 million customers on MTN's MoMo platform have zero access to personalised financial guidance. Their decisions about saving, spending, and transferring money are made without any feedback loop. A customer who consistently depletes their wallet before month-end has no mechanism to learn this pattern. Financial coaching exists in the abstract (financial literacy campaigns) but never at the individual transaction level where behaviour change actually happens.
Problem 2 — Low MoMo Engagement Among Casual Users
Approximately 40% of registered MoMo customers are classified as "casual" users — fewer than 4 transactions per month. These customers have a MoMo wallet but do not integrate it deeply into their financial lives. The absence of a compelling reason to check the wallet, set goals, or track progress means MoMo is used opportunistically. A personalised financial health score creates a weekly pull mechanism that brings customers back to the platform.
Problem 3 — Cross-Sell Conversion Is Low Because Customers Are Not Ready
MTN Fintech's credit, insurance, and savings products have low conversion rates because customers receive offers without context. The financial health coach builds an advisory relationship over weeks and months, making product offers feel like recommendations from a trusted advisor rather than unsolicited marketing. Coached customers show 15–20% higher cross-sell conversion in comparable programmes.

4. User Stories

US-01 · Financial Health Score Check
Customer Checks Their Score via USSD
"As a MoMo customer, I want to dial a code and see my financial health score so I can understand how I am doing financially."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Score displayed (e.g. "Your score: 68/100 — Good") within 160 chars
  • Score computed from last 30 days MoMo data within 3 seconds
  • Top 1 strength and top 1 improvement area shown
  • Option to explore spending, savings, income sub-scores
US-02 · Weekly SMS Nudge
Personalised Weekly Coaching Nudge
"As a MoMo customer, I want to receive a weekly SMS that tells me something specific and actionable about my financial behaviour."
Acceptance Criteria
  • SMS sent every Monday at 08:00 to opted-in customers
  • Message personalised to individual's actual MoMo data (not generic)
  • Message le 160 characters (single SMS unit)
  • Reply STOP to opt out, takes effect within 24 hours
US-03 · Spending Category Breakdown
View Spending by Category This Month
"As a MoMo customer, I want to see where my money went this month broken down by category over USSD."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Top 3 spending categories shown (Airtime: 35%, Food: 28%, Transfers: 22%)
  • Comparison to last month shown ("Airtime up 12% vs last month")
  • Paginated within 160-char USSD format
  • Category classification accuracy ge 85%
US-04 · Savings Goal Setting
Set and Track a Savings Goal via USSD
"As a MoMo customer, I want to set a savings goal (save R500 by month-end) and track my progress weekly via nudges."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Customer enters goal amount and target date via USSD
  • Weekly progress shown as percentage in SMS nudge
  • Celebration message on goal completion
  • Goal can be updated or cancelled at any time via USSD
US-05 · Income Regularity Insight
Coach Identifies Irregular Income Pattern
"As a customer with irregular income, I want the coach to recognise my pattern and give advice tailored to income volatility."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Income classified: Regular / Irregular / Seasonal
  • Irregular income customers receive specific buffer-building advice
  • Advice references actual average income from MoMo data
  • Not generic: "Your income varied 45% over 3 months. Build a 2-week buffer."
US-06 · Multilingual Coaching
Receive Coaching in Preferred Language
"As a Zulu-speaking customer, I want my weekly nudge and USSD score screen to be in Zulu, not English."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Customer sets language preference at opt-in (stored in profile)
  • All nudge templates translated to supported languages
  • Language can be changed at any time via USSD menu
  • Currency and number formatting correct per locale
US-07 · Score Trend Tracking
Track Score Improvement Over 3 Months
"As a coached customer, I want to see how my financial health score has changed over the last 3 months to see my progress."
Acceptance Criteria
  • 3-month score trend: "Your score: 68 (+8 from 3 months ago)"
  • Trend direction indicated (up/down/stable) in text
  • Key factor driving improvement highlighted
  • Monthly scores stored without retaining raw transaction data
US-08 · POPIA Consent at Opt-In
First-Use Data Processing Consent
"As a customer, I want to consent to my MoMo transaction data being used for coaching before any analysis occurs."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Consent prompt shown on first coach feature access
  • Clearly describes what data is used (MoMo transactions only)
  • Decline routes to standard MoMo menu; no analysis occurs
  • Consent timestamp stored immutably in MoMo profile

6. Technical Architecture

Customer Channel
Feature Phone → USSD Gateway → Coach USSD Application Server (FastAPI)  |  SMS Gateway → Customer Mobile
Score Engine
Daily Batch: MoMo Data Lake → Feature Engineering (Spark) → Dimension Scorers → Composite Score Computation → Score Store (Redis per-market)
Nudge Generation
Weekly Batch (Sunday night): Score + Context Retrieval → LLM Nudge Generator (Claude claude-sonnet-4-6, 160-char enforcer) → Language Translator → SMS Queue (Kafka)
Data & ML
Transaction Classifier (ML, merchant codes + amounts) → Income Regularity Detector → Savings Rate Calculator → MLflow (model versioning) → Monthly model evaluation
Compliance & Ops
POPIA Consent Store → Opt-Out Manager → Data Minimisation Layer (scores only, no raw transaction retention) → Audit Log (Kafka) → Grafana Dashboard

Nudge Template Design

Next Step: Design 160-Character Personalised Insight Templates
The first design task for UC-27 is to create a library of personalised nudge templates covering all combinations of: score tier (Poor/Fair/Good/Excellent), dimension in focus (savings/spending/income/engagement), trend (improving/declining/stable), and goal status (set/not set/complete). Each template must be le 160 characters, reference at least one real data point from the customer's MoMo history, and be translatable to 6+ languages without exceeding the character limit. Estimated: 80–120 base templates covering 90%+ of customer scenarios.
