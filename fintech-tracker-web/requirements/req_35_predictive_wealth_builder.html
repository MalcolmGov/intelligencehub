Executive Summary & Business Case

MTN Group Fintech serves over 65 million active MoMo subscribers across 13 African markets, yet none of these customers currently have access to investment products through MoMo. The traditional wealth management and investment industry in Africa has minimum investment thresholds that exclude the vast majority of the continent's working population — often requiring R10,000–R50,000 to open a retail investment account. UC-35 eliminates this barrier entirely.

The Predictive Wealth Builder embeds AI-powered micro-investment automation directly into the MoMo consumer app, enabling any subscriber to invest from as little as R1 (or local currency equivalent). The product uses behavioural AI to analyse transaction patterns, identify discretionary spending capacity, and automatically route micro-amounts into government bonds, ETFs, and tax-free savings accounts — without the customer needing to actively think about it.

The core mechanic is round-up investing: every MoMo purchase is rounded up to the nearest rand/naira/shilling, with the difference automatically invested according to the customer's chosen goal and risk profile. Supplementary features include goal-based savings pots (school fees, emergency fund, home deposit), AI portfolio rebalancing, and tax-free savings account (TFSA) integration where regulatory frameworks permit.

The business case is anchored on new fee income streams: an annual management fee of 0.5–1.5% AUM, transaction fees on ETF purchases, and premium tier subscription fees for advanced AI features. At 5 million investors averaging R500 AUM, fee income reaches R37.5M annually at 1.5% AMF. Crucially, this product increases customer lifetime value, reduces churn, and positions MoMo as a full-spectrum financial services provider rather than a payments utility.

Problem Statement

Africa has one of the youngest and fastest-growing populations on earth, with a burgeoning middle class that has demonstrated strong financial discipline through consistent mobile money usage. Yet structural barriers in the traditional investment industry systematically exclude these customers from wealth creation. Three compounding problems define the gap:

Problem 1 — Minimum Thresholds Exclude the Majority
Regulated investment products in South Africa, Nigeria, Kenya and other MTN markets require minimum initial investments that are out of reach for the majority of MoMo's subscriber base. A market trader who saves R50 per week and uses MoMo daily has no viable path to compound wealth accumulation. The result is a structural wealth gap that mobile money payments have not addressed. UC-35 solves this with a R1 minimum investment, the lowest in any regulated African investment product.
Problem 2 — MoMo Is Untapped as a Wealth Platform
MoMo captures daily transaction data for 65 million subscribers — spending patterns, income cadence, discretionary capacity, seasonal fluctuations. This is precisely the data a robo-advisor uses to build an investment profile. Yet MTN currently monetises this data only through payments fees. UC-35 converts transactional data into an investment intelligence engine, generating fee income while delivering genuine financial uplift to subscribers. Competitors (M-Pesa, OPay, Airtel Money) are moving into investment products; MTN must act in 2026 or cede this market.
Problem 3 — Manual Investing Requires Financial Literacy Most Customers Lack
Traditional investment products require customers to actively select funds, understand risk ratings, rebalance portfolios, and manage tax implications. The majority of MTN's subscriber base lacks the financial literacy to navigate these decisions confidently. UC-35's AI removes this burden entirely — the system makes portfolio decisions, executes round-ups automatically, rebalances monthly, and surfaces only the information the customer needs to stay engaged with their goals. The product is designed for financial inclusion by design, not as an afterthought.

User Stories

US-01 · Round-Up Investing
Automatic Round-Up on Every Purchase
"As a MoMo user, I want every purchase I make to automatically round up to the nearest rand and invest the difference, so I can build savings without thinking about it."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Round-up calculated on every MoMo debit transaction (minimum R0.01)
  • Round-up amounts batched daily and invested by 23:00
  • Customer can pause/resume round-ups from app at any time
  • Round-up multiplier (1x, 2x, 3x) configurable by customer
  • Daily round-up summary notification sent via push/SMS
US-02 · Goal-Based Savings Pots
Create and Track Named Investment Goals
"As a parent, I want to create a savings pot called 'School Fees 2027' with a target amount and date, and have the AI tell me if I'm on track."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Customer can create up to 5 named goals with target amount and target date
  • AI calculates required monthly contribution to reach goal on time
  • Progress bar and days-remaining displayed per goal
  • AI sends proactive alerts if customer is falling behind target
  • Goal funds ring-fenced in separate allocation within portfolio
US-03 · ETF & Bond Access
Invest in Exchange-Traded Funds and Government Bonds
"As a first-time investor, I want to invest in ETFs and government bonds directly from MoMo without needing a stockbroker account."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Minimum 3 ETF options (equity, balanced, fixed income) available in-app
  • Government retail bonds available where JSE/NSE infrastructure supports
  • Plain-language fund descriptions with risk rating (1-5)
  • Historical performance chart displayed (1M, 3M, 1Y, 3Y)
  • Purchase executes via licensed asset manager partner within T+2
US-04 · AI Portfolio Rebalancing
Automatic Monthly Portfolio Optimisation
"As an investor, I want my portfolio to be automatically rebalanced every month so I don't have to manually adjust my allocations as markets move."
Acceptance Criteria
  • AI rebalances portfolio to target allocation on the 1st of each month
  • Rebalancing triggered if any asset class drifts >5% from target
  • Customer receives rebalancing summary notification post-execution
  • Customer can opt out of auto-rebalancing and manage manually
  • Tax-aware rebalancing (avoid unnecessary capital gains events)
US-05 · Risk Profiling
AI-Assisted Risk Assessment at Onboarding
"As a new investor, I want to complete a short questionnaire and have the AI recommend a portfolio that suits my financial situation and risk appetite."
Acceptance Criteria
  • 5-question onboarding flow completed in under 2 minutes
  • AI assigns risk profile (Conservative / Moderate / Growth / Aggressive)
  • Recommended portfolio allocation displayed with rationale
  • Customer can override AI recommendation before confirming
  • Risk profile stored and used for all subsequent investment decisions
US-06 · Tax-Free Savings Account
TFSA Wrapper for SA Investors
"As a South African MoMo user, I want my investments to be held in a Tax-Free Savings Account wrapper to maximise tax efficiency."
Acceptance Criteria
  • TFSA product available to SA-market customers (SARS registered)
  • Annual contribution limit (R36,000) tracked and enforced in-app
  • Lifetime limit (R500,000) displayed on TFSA dashboard
  • System prevents contributions exceeding SARS annual limit
  • Annual tax certificate generated and downloadable in-app
US-07 · Spending Intelligence
AI Identifies Discretionary Capacity for Investing
"As a MoMo user, I want the AI to analyse my spending and tell me how much I can safely invest each month without affecting my essential expenses."
Acceptance Criteria
  • AI analyses 90-day MoMo transaction history to identify income and expense patterns
  • Discretionary capacity calculated as income minus essential spend minus buffer (20%)
  • Monthly investment recommendation surfaced in-app with explanation
  • Customer can accept, adjust, or reject AI recommendation
  • Recommendation updates monthly based on latest 90-day transaction data
US-08 · Withdrawal
Partial and Full Portfolio Withdrawal
"As an investor, I want to withdraw part of my investment back to my MoMo wallet in an emergency without losing my entire portfolio."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Partial withdrawal available for liquid assets (money market, ETFs) within T+3
  • Minimum withdrawal amount R10 (or local currency equivalent)
  • Tax implications displayed before withdrawal confirmation
  • Withdrawn funds credited to MoMo wallet within 3 business days
  • Goal impact warning shown if withdrawal affects goal achievement

Data & AI Model Requirements

7.1 Input Data Sources

MoMo Transaction History
90-day rolling window of all debit/credit transactions per customer. Used for spending pattern analysis, income identification, discretionary capacity calculation, and round-up triggering. Minimum 30-day history required for AI recommendation activation.
Customer Risk Profile Data
5-question onboarding questionnaire responses: investment goal, time horizon, risk tolerance, income stability, emergency fund status. Used to assign risk band and generate initial portfolio recommendation. Refreshed every 24 months.
Market Price Data
15-minute delayed ETF NAV from JSE/NSE data feeds. Government bond yields from central bank APIs. Money market rates from asset manager partners. Used for portfolio valuation, rebalancing calculations, and return reporting.
Goal Configuration Data
Customer-defined goal name, target amount, target date, priority ranking. Used by goal projection engine to calculate required monthly contribution, assess on-track status, and generate proactive alerts.
Portfolio Ledger Data
Real-time portfolio holdings: instrument code, units held, average purchase price, current value, unrealised P&L, asset class allocation. Used for rebalancing decisions, withdrawal calculations, and statement generation.
Macro Economic Signals (Phase 2)
CPI data, prime rate, JSE ALSI trend. Used by advanced rebalancing model to adjust equity/bond split in response to macro environment. Phase 2 feature requiring additional data licences.

7.3 Data Privacy & Governance

  • All transaction data used for AI analysis is processed within the customer's home market data boundary (POPIA — SA; NDPR — Nigeria; Kenya DPA — Kenya)
  • AI processing of financial transaction data for investment recommendations constitutes "further processing" under POPIA — explicit consent obtained at investment onboarding
  • No raw transaction data shared with asset manager partners — only aggregated portfolio instructions transmitted
  • Customer has right to data access, correction, and deletion per POPIA Section 23 — deletion request triggers investment account closure workflow
  • Model training data must be anonymised — MSISDN replaced with pseudonymous customer ID before any model training pipeline ingestion

FSP Licence / Distribution Partner Strategy
MTN Group Fintech does not currently hold a Financial Services Provider (FSP) licence under FAIS. UC-35 Phase 1 will be delivered via a Section 13B distribution agreement with a licensed asset manager partner (preferred: Satrix, 10X Investments, or similar). This agreement allows MTN to distribute the partner's CIS products through the MoMo app without holding its own FSP licence. Phase 2 will evaluate the business case for MTN obtaining its own FSP licence as AUM grows. The distribution partner model must be reflected in all T&Cs, key information documents (KID), and regulatory filings.
Next Steps: Regulatory & Partnership
