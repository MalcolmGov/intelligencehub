Executive Summary

The Regulatory Change Impact Radar is an AI-powered intelligence platform that continuously monitors 6+ South African regulatory bodies — SARB, FSCA, POPIA Information Regulator, PCI-SSC and FICA — for amendments, new guidelines, consultation papers and enforcement notices. Changes are auto-classified by impact severity, mapped to affected internal systems and processes, and surfaced to compliance and legal teams as board-ready impact assessments with implementation timelines and cost estimates.

Malcolm Govender identified this as a critical strategic gap: the organisation currently monitors regulatory bodies manually and reactively, with compliance teams spending significant time reading regulatory publications and manually assessing their impact. This creates a risk of missed regulatory changes and slow response times that expose the organisation to enforcement action.

Problem Statement

Manual Monitoring of 6+ Regulatory Bodies
Compliance and legal teams manually monitor SARB, FSCA, POPIA Information Regulator, PCI-SSC, FICA and NCR publications. This requires dedicated time across multiple portals, email subscriptions and PDF-heavy regulatory documents — creating a high-effort, error-prone process.
Weeks-Long Regulatory Response Cycle
From a regulatory change being published to an internal impact assessment landing on the board's desk currently takes 4–8 weeks. In a fast-moving regulatory environment, this lag creates enforcement risk and increases remediation cost as systems continue operating out of compliance.
No Systematic Impact-to-System Mapping
When a regulatory change is identified, mapping it to affected systems, processes and business units is done manually through tribal knowledge. There is no maintained map of regulatory obligations to the technical system landscape, making impact assessment inconsistent and incomplete.
Board Reporting Requires Manual Synthesis
Preparing board-level regulatory impact summaries with implementation timelines and cost estimates requires significant manual effort from senior compliance staff. There is no automated mechanism to produce audit-ready impact assessments on demand.

Solution Overview

The Regulatory Change Impact Radar operates as a three-component intelligence platform: a regulatory feed ingestion layer that monitors official sources in real time, an AI impact classification engine that assesses severity and maps changes to internal systems, and a reporting layer that generates board-ready assessments and maintains the living regulatory obligation register.

Core Capabilities

Real-Time Regulatory Feed Monitoring
Automated ingestion of regulatory publications via RSS feeds, website scraping with change detection, email parsing and official API integrations where available. New publications trigger immediate analysis pipeline — no manual checking required.
AI Impact Classification Engine
LLM-powered analysis classifies each regulatory change by: impact severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low / Informational), affected obligation category (data privacy, AML/CFT, payment systems, conduct of business, capital requirements), implementation timeline and estimated cost range.
System-to-Obligation Mapping
Regulatory changes are automatically mapped to affected internal systems, processes and business units using a maintained regulatory obligation register. Engineers and business owners are notified of changes affecting their domain.
Board-Ready Impact Assessment Generation
Automated generation of structured impact assessment documents: executive summary, affected systems, gap analysis, implementation roadmap with timelines, resource requirements and cost estimates. Ready for legal review and board distribution within hours.

Technical Architecture

Feed Ingestion Layer (Python)
Scheduled crawlers per regulatory source using RSS parsers, HTML diff-detection scrapers and email parsing. Change deduplication using content hashing. New publication events published to internal message queue for downstream processing. Configurable polling intervals per source.
AI Classification Engine (Python + Azure OpenAI)
GPT-4o powered document analysis pipeline. Structured output schema enforces consistent classification fields. Domain-specific few-shot prompting built from historical regulatory change examples. Expert review feedback loop for continuous accuracy improvement.
Regulatory Obligation Register (PostgreSQL)
Normalised data model linking regulations to obligations, obligations to internal systems, and systems to responsible teams. Updated by both automated classification and manual compliance team input. Exposes REST API for dashboard and reporting consumption.
Impact Assessment Generator (TypeScript)
Template-driven document generator producing board-ready PDFs and structured JSON. Templates maintained per regulatory body to ensure appropriate formatting and terminology. Legal team review workflow integrated before final distribution.
Notification & Routing Layer
Configurable routing rules mapping severity + obligation category to stakeholder groups. Multi-channel delivery: email, Slack, Microsoft Teams. Escalation logic for unacknowledged Critical alerts. Delivery receipts tracked for audit trail.
