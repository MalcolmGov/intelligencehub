1. Executive Summary & Business Case

Approximately 1.4 billion adults across sub-Saharan Africa remain excluded from formal credit markets. The primary barrier is not creditworthiness — it is the absence of a credit history that traditional bureaux can score. MTN's Mobile Money platform, however, generates a rich, longitudinal dataset for every registered customer: transaction frequency, average balances, merchant payment behaviour, airtime recharge patterns, utility payments, and peer transfer networks. This data constitutes a powerful alternative credit signal that traditional lenders cannot access.

UC-26 builds an AI-powered credit scoring and micro-lending engine that operates entirely within the USSD channel — accessible to any GSM handset, no internet required. A customer dials a short code, requests a loan amount, and receives an instant credit decision within 3 seconds. If approved, funds are disbursed directly to their MoMo wallet. Repayment is automated via scheduled MoMo wallet deductions. No paperwork, no branch visit, no credit bureau check required.

The lending business model generates high-margin revenue: micro-loans carry interest rates of 8–24% per month depending on risk tier, with a projected portfolio NPL rate of 6–9% manageable through automated repayment and conservative initial limits. At 500,000 active borrowers with an average outstanding balance of R800, the loan book reaches R400 million with annual interest revenue approaching R600 million at blended rates. Even at conservative penetration of 2% of MTN's 65M MoMo customers, the financial inclusion and revenue impact is transformational.

2. Problem Statement

Three interlocking problems create the opportunity for UC-26:

Problem 1 — The Credit Invisibility Trap
Across MTN's 13 markets, the majority of Mobile Money customers have no formal credit history. Without a credit bureau score, they are invisible to traditional lenders. Yet many of these customers demonstrate consistent, reliable financial behaviour through their MoMo wallets: regular income deposits, timely airtime purchases, consistent merchant payments, and prudent balance management. The credit invisibility trap prevents financially responsible people from accessing the credit they need to smooth income volatility, manage emergencies, and invest in livelihoods.
Problem 2 — Emergency Liquidity Gaps Drive Harmful Alternatives
When MTN customers face unexpected expenses — medical bills, school fees, transport costs, equipment repair — they currently turn to informal lenders (mashonisas/loan sharks) charging effective annual rates of 300–600%, or they sell productive assets at distressed prices. The absence of a fast, affordable emergency credit product pushes financially vulnerable customers into cycles of debt or asset loss. MTN's USSD channel can close this gap with a regulated, fairly-priced alternative.
Problem 3 — MTN's MoMo Data Is an Untapped Credit Asset
MTN possesses one of the richest longitudinal financial behaviour datasets on the continent. Every airtime recharge, peer transfer, merchant payment, utility bill, and savings deposit represents a data point about a customer's financial capacity and reliability. This data is currently used for product analytics and marketing, but not for credit origination. UC-26 monetises this asset directly while simultaneously delivering genuine financial inclusion value.

4. User Stories

US-01 · First Loan Application
Feature-Phone Loan via USSD
"As a MoMo customer who has never accessed formal credit, I want to apply for a small loan using my feature phone and receive a decision instantly."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Customer dials USSD short code, requests R500 loan
  • Credit score computed from MoMo history within 3 seconds
  • Approved customers see offer with amount, rate, and repayment date
  • Funds disbursed to MoMo wallet within 30 seconds of acceptance
US-02 · Credit Score Transparency
Customer Views Their Credit Score
"As a MoMo customer, I want to see my credit score and understand what factors are improving or reducing it."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Score (0–100) displayed with tier label (Poor/Fair/Good/Excellent)
  • Top 2 positive factors shown within 160-char USSD format
  • Top 1 improvement area shown (e.g. "Top up airtime more regularly")
  • Score refreshes daily based on latest MoMo activity
US-03 · Automated Repayment
Repayment Deducted Automatically on Due Date
"As a borrower, I want my repayment to be automatically deducted from my MoMo wallet on the due date without any manual action."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Deduction attempted at 08:00 on repayment due date
  • If insufficient balance: retry at 12:00, 18:00, 22:00
  • SMS reminder sent 48 hours before due date
  • Successful deduction confirmed via SMS to borrower
US-04 · Loan Declined — Reason Given
Declined Applicant Receives Actionable Guidance
"As a customer whose loan was declined, I want to understand why and what I can do to qualify in future."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Decline reason shown (score too low / existing loan / KYC tier)
  • Top 1 actionable improvement suggestion provided
  • Next eligible application date shown if applicable
  • All within 160 characters per USSD message
US-05 · Loan History
Borrower Checks Outstanding Loan Status
"As a borrower, I want to check my outstanding loan balance, due date, and repayment history via USSD."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Outstanding balance, interest, and due date displayed
  • Last 3 repayments shown in paginated USSD format
  • Option to make early repayment from within USSD session
  • Early repayment improves credit score immediately
US-06 · Risk Officer Override
Credit Risk Officer Reviews Model Output
"As a credit risk officer, I want to review the AI credit score model's feature importances and override decisions for specific customer segments."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Risk dashboard shows feature importance per score decile
  • Manual override capability for individual MSISDNs
  • Override audit log maintained (who, why, timestamp)
  • Segment-level risk appetite parameter configuration
US-07 · NCR Compliance Disclosure
Mandatory Credit Disclosure Before Acceptance
"As a regulator, I want every borrower to see the full cost of credit (principal, interest, fees, total repayable) before accepting a loan."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Pre-acceptance screen shows: principal, monthly rate, total repayable, due date
  • Customer must press 1 to accept or 2 to decline
  • No loan disbursed without explicit acceptance recorded
  • Acceptance timestamp and loan terms stored immutably
US-08 · Top-Up Loan
Existing Good-Standing Borrower Requests Limit Increase
"As a borrower with a clean repayment history, I want my loan limit to increase automatically over time."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Credit limit reviewed monthly for on-time payers
  • After 3 successful repayments: limit increases 25–50%
  • SMS notification sent when limit increases
  • Limit increase reflected immediately in USSD credit check

FR-01The credit scoring model SHALL compute a 0–100 score for each eligible MoMo customer using at minimum the following features: (a) 12-month MoMo transaction volume and value, (b) airtime recharge frequency and consistency, (c) utility payment behaviour, (d) network tenure (months as MTN subscriber), (e) average wallet balance trend, (f) merchant payment diversity.MUSTNo credit bureau data required

Data & ML
MoMo Transaction Data Lake (Kafka ingestion, Spark ETL) → Feature Store (Redis, real-time features) → MLflow (model versioning, evaluation) → Monthly model retraining pipeline
Loan Management & Repayment
Loan Management System (LMS) → MoMo API (disbursement + collection) → SMS Gateway (reminders) → Collections Service (arrears management) → NCR Reporting
Compliance & Ops
NCR Disclosure Engine → Audit Log (Kafka, immutable) → Risk Officer Dashboard → Prometheus + Grafana (monitoring) → Market-local data residency (per OpCo)

Model Governance

Model: XGBoost Gradient Boosting Classifier
Algorithm selected for: (a) high accuracy on tabular financial data, (b) native SHAP support for explainability, (c) fast inference (<50ms per scoring call), (d) proven performance in African MFS credit scoring contexts (M-Shwari, similar). Monthly retraining on 18-month rolling window. Evaluation metric: Gini coefficient + KS statistic + expected NPL rate.
Next Steps: Define Credit Scoring Model Inputs
The first task for UC-26 is to convene a credit risk workshop with: (1) MTN Fintech Risk Officer, (2) MoMo data engineering team, (3) a credit risk consultant with African MFS experience. Output: agreed feature list, data availability confirmation, risk appetite framework (maximum NPL rate per market, minimum score thresholds, loan size limits by tier).
