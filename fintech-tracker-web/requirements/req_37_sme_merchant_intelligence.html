1. Executive Summary & Business Case

MTN Group Fintech has built a significant merchant network through MoMo POS and merchant wallet products across its 13 African markets. These merchants — predominantly small and medium enterprises (SMEs) ranging from individual market traders to small retail shops — generate daily transaction data through MoMo but receive nothing in return except a payment receipt. They have no access to business analytics, no financial reporting, and no intelligence to help them grow. Most cannot afford a bookkeeper, let alone a CFO.

UC-37 changes this equation dramatically. By converting raw MoMo POS and merchant wallet transaction data into an AI-powered business intelligence layer, MTN gives every merchant a free AI CFO embedded in the MoMo merchant app. The system generates daily, weekly, and monthly P&L summaries, predicts when specific product categories will run out of stock, identifies peak trading hours for staffing optimisation, benchmarks the merchant against similar businesses in the same area, and surfaces targeted promotional recommendations that have the highest probability of driving revenue.

The revenue model is two-pronged: (1) Revenue-Based Lending (RBL) — the AI intelligence layer generates a real-time lending eligibility score based on merchant revenue patterns, enabling instant working capital loans repaid as a percentage of daily MoMo sales, and (2) Premium Intelligence Subscriptions — advanced analytics features (competitor benchmarking, supplier marketplace, multi-location management) available for a monthly fee. Merchants who grow their business through MoMo intelligence are also more likely to increase their MoMo transaction volume, driving fee income.

2. Problem Statement

Problem 1 — African SMEs Are Flying Blind on Business Performance
The majority of MoMo's 2 million+ merchants are micro and small businesses with no access to accounting software, business intelligence tools, or financial advisors. They make inventory decisions based on intuition, miss their own peak trading hours, price products without competitive intelligence, and have no idea whether their business is growing or shrinking in real terms. MoMo holds the data to solve all of these problems but does not surface it to merchants.
Problem 2 — SMEs Are Excluded from Traditional Business Lending
African SMEs are routinely denied traditional bank loans due to lack of formal financial records, no audited accounts, and insufficient collateral. The irony is that MoMo holds precisely the revenue evidence that banks require — in real-time, unmanipulated form. Revenue-Based Lending (RBL) using MoMo revenue as the underwriting basis is the most natural credit product for this segment, but requires the AI intelligence layer to accurately score lending eligibility and calibrate repayment structures.
Problem 3 — Merchant Churn Risk from Super-App Competitors
Super-apps (OPay, PalmPay, Chipper Cash) are aggressively targeting African SME merchants with bundled payment + analytics + credit offers. MTN MoMo's competitive moat is its 13-market network and brand trust, but without meaningful merchant value-add beyond payments, churn risk is increasing. UC-37 deepens merchant stickiness by making MoMo the irreplaceable business intelligence backbone of the merchant's operation — not just a payment terminal.

4. User Stories

US-01 · Daily P&L Summary
Automated Daily Business Performance Report
"As a MoMo merchant, I want to see my daily revenue, number of transactions, average basket size, and comparison to yesterday at a glance so I know how my business is performing."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Daily P&L summary generated and available by 06:00 each morning
  • Shows: total revenue, transaction count, avg basket, vs previous day, vs 7-day avg
  • Top 3 revenue-generating product categories highlighted
  • Summary available in app and via WhatsApp (opted-in merchants)
  • Displayed in merchant's local currency with correct formatting
US-02 · Stock-Out Prediction
AI Predicts When Stock Will Run Out
"As a spaza shop owner, I want the app to tell me when I'm likely to run out of bread, milk, and airtime so I can reorder in time."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Stock-out prediction generated per product category based on sales velocity
  • Alert sent 3 days before predicted stock-out (configurable by merchant)
  • Supplier marketplace link embedded in stock-out alert
  • Prediction accuracy: >75% within ±2 days on validation set
  • Seasonal adjustment applied (e.g., higher demand around month-end)
US-03 · Peak Hour Analysis
Identify Busiest Trading Hours
"As a food court vendor, I want to know what hours I'm busiest so I know when to hire extra staff and when I can take breaks."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Hourly transaction heatmap displayed per day of week
  • Peak hours highlighted with "Your busiest hour: 12:00-13:00" insight
  • Slowest hours identified with "Consider a lunch promotion" suggestion
  • Data based on minimum 30-day rolling transaction history
  • Heatmap exportable as image for staff scheduling use
US-04 · Revenue-Based Lending
Instant Working Capital Loan Pre-Approval
"As a merchant growing my stock, I want to be offered a working capital loan based on my MoMo sales history without needing bank statements or collateral."
Acceptance Criteria
  • RBL eligibility score calculated daily for all active merchants (>90 days history)
  • Pre-approved loan amount displayed in-app if score exceeds threshold
  • Repayment structured as % of daily MoMo sales (configurable 5–20%)
  • Merchant can accept loan offer in-app in under 3 minutes
  • Funds disbursed to MoMo merchant wallet within 24 hours of acceptance
US-05 · WhatsApp Daily Report
Business Summary Delivered via WhatsApp
"As a busy merchant, I want my daily business summary sent to my WhatsApp every morning so I can check it without opening the app."
Acceptance Criteria
  • WhatsApp Business API integration delivers formatted daily summary
  • Report delivered by 07:00 daily to opted-in merchants
  • Merchant can reply "MORE" to get weekly comparison, "STOP" to unsubscribe
  • Report includes yesterday's revenue, top product, one AI insight
  • Delivered in merchant's preferred language (English, French, Swahili, Hausa)
US-06 · Competitor Benchmarking
Anonymous Benchmark vs Similar Merchants
"As a merchant, I want to know how my sales compare to similar shops in my area so I can understand if I'm growing or losing market share."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Merchant benchmarked against anonymised peers: same area, same category, similar size
  • Benchmark shows percentile rank: "Your revenue is in the top 40% of similar shops"
  • Minimum peer group size of 10 merchants before benchmark displayed (privacy)
  • Individual merchant data never identifiable in benchmark calculations
  • Available in premium subscription tier only
US-07 · Promotion Recommendations
AI-Generated Promotion Suggestions
"As a merchant, I want the AI to suggest promotions based on my slow-moving stock and upcoming slow periods so I can drive revenue when I need it most."
Acceptance Criteria
  • AI identifies slow-moving product categories (velocity below 30-day avg)
  • Generates specific promotion suggestion: "Run a 10% discount on cooking oil this weekend based on your Thursday–Sunday slow period"
  • Merchant can activate promotion in-app with one tap
  • Post-promotion sales lift measured and reported to merchant
  • Maximum 1 promotion suggestion per day to avoid alert fatigue
US-08 · Monthly Financial Statement
Auto-Generated Monthly Business Statement
"As a merchant who needs to apply for a lease renewal, I want a professional monthly statement of my MoMo business revenue that I can share with my landlord."
Acceptance Criteria
  • Monthly statement generated in PDF format by the 3rd of each month
  • Shows: revenue by week, top products, transaction count, average basket
  • MTN MoMo branding and merchant business name on statement
  • Downloadable and shareable from app
  • Statement covers prior calendar month; available for up to 12 months history

7. Data & AI Model Requirements

The intelligence layer requires a minimum 90-day transaction history per merchant before the full feature set is activated. For new merchants, the following progressive activation applies:

Days ActiveFeatures UnlockedAI Models Active
Day 1–14Basic transaction log, daily revenue totalNone (insufficient data)
Day 15–44Daily P&L summary, peak hour heatmap (limited)Sales aggregation only
Day 45–89Full P&L, peak hours, category breakdown, WhatsApp reportsStock-out predictor (limited), category classifier
Day 90+All features including RBL eligibility, benchmarking, promotion engineAll models fully active

Data Privacy & POPIA/NDPR Compliance

  • Merchant transaction data processed per POPIA (SA), NDPR (Nigeria), Kenya DPA for analytics purposes
  • Peer benchmarking uses anonymised, aggregated data only — individual merchant data never identifiable in benchmark output
  • Merchant consents to AI-powered analytics at merchant onboarding (embedded in MoMo merchant T&Cs)
  • WhatsApp delivery uses encrypted WhatsApp Business API — no transaction data stored by Meta
  • RBL credit decision process follows NCA prescribed disclosure and right-to-reason obligations
  • POPIA impact assessment required before launch covering both merchant data processing and benchmarking aggregation
